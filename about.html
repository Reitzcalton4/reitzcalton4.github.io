About me

A practical, community-centred approach to conservation

Forestry professional focused on restoration, indigenous trees, and environmental stewardship.

I am a trained forestry professional with a Diploma in Forestry from Kenya Forestry College, awarded with Distinction. My interests and practical work focus on restoring degraded forest landscapes, supporting indigenous tree production, and working with communities and conservation partners to protect natural resources.

My conservation experience includes work connected with the rehabilitation of the Kakamega Tropical Rainforest landscape, tree nursery activities, indigenous seedling production, site preparation, planting support, and follow-up tending.

I value practical fieldwork, collaboration, responsible management of natural resources, and long-term care of restored sites.

Professional mission: To help restore degraded landscapes, conserve biodiversity, strengthen community stewardship, and support sustainable management of Kenya’s forest resources.
